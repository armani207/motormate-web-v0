Company positioning

A pediatric rehab-tech company building hardware, data, and network infrastructure.

MotorMate Health exists to commercialize practical cerebral palsy technology: a sensor-assisted rehab device, sourced CP awareness metrics, and a digital networking layer for the CP ecosystem. Support from NSHSS and Oakland County helped move the work from prototype concept toward a pilot-ready business platform.

Business origin

Started as device IP. Expanded into a market access system.

MotorMate began as a student-built sensor prototype. The commercial direction is now broader: hardware sales, pilot validation, catalog-driven community acquisition, and trusted digital networking for CP stakeholders.

What the company is building

  • Product layer: MotorMate device, firmware, activity loops, and session analytics.
  • Network layer: Searchable CP Catalog for families, clinicians, researchers, and partners.
  • Evidence layer: Pilot sites, technical validation, business development, and commercialization.

Execution principles

Build like a health-tech business, not a hobby project.

  • Use privacy controls because the catalog is a networking asset, not a public data dump.
  • Package the device as a pilot-ready product with measurable workflows.
  • Separate awareness data, product telemetry, and clinical claims.
  • Convert community interest into partnerships, pilots, and customer discovery.
