About MotorMate Health

We exist to make cerebral palsy care more connected, compassionate, and data‑informed. Starting with innovation grants from NSHSS and Oakland County, Michigan we built a platform to connect families, clinicians, and advocates across the U.S. and beyond.

Our Story

MotorMate began as a school project to prototype playful, sensor‑powered exercises for children with mild–moderate CP. As families and clinicians shared feedback, we expanded into a community platform with a live CP Catalog, secure intake, and transparent roadmap.

  • Google‑based intake forms for fast scale-up
  • De‑identified analytics with consent‑first workflows
  • Partnerships with marketing & product leaders to support outreach and pilots

What We Do

  • Connect: Maintain a searchable CP Catalog for families, clinicians, and advocates.
  • Innovate: Build the MotorMate device with clinician‑guided goals and kid‑friendly game loops.
  • Collaborate: Partner with schools, clinics, universities, and startups for pilots and studies.
