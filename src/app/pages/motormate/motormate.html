MotorMate device

Sensor-assisted rehab hardware for a measurable therapy pipeline.

MotorMate is a patent-pending device concept for upper-limb motor practice in children with mild-to-moderate cerebral palsy. The product package is hardware, firmware, guided activities, and session analytics built for pilots, clinics, and future commercialization.

Technical value proposition

A device stack built around capture, engagement, and reporting.

MotorMate needs to work as a therapy-facing product, not a demo. The device must capture usable movement signals, support repeatable sessions, and produce summaries a care team can evaluate.

1

Hardware-assisted reps

The device translates upper-limb practice into structured movement tasks with sensor capture.

2

Session telemetry

The data model should summarize repetitions, range, consistency, completion, and engagement.

3

Clinic-to-home workflow

Short routines should support clinician-directed goals while remaining usable outside the clinic.

Operational workflow

Designed for deployment, not just demonstration.

Configure

A clinician or caregiver selects the target movement and calibrates the session parameters.

Run session

The child completes guided repetitions while the device records movement and completion signals.

Export insight

The system packages session output into reviewable summaries for pilots and care conversations.

Commercial validation roadmap

From prototype to product-readiness evidence.

  • Prototype stack: Validate sensing, firmware behavior, enclosure ergonomics, and game loops.
  • User testing: Measure setup friction, repeatability, engagement, and caregiver comprehension.
  • Pilot packaging: Evaluate telemetry, workflow fit, reporting format, and clinical usefulness.
  • Commercial review: Prepare durability, cost, compliance, and production partnership planning.
