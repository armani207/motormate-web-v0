MotorMate — Precision, Play & Progress 🧠⚙️

MotorMate is a patent‑pending, sensor‑powered therapy device designed to support upper‑limb motor goals for children with mild–moderate cerebral palsy. It blends calibrated exercises with game loops to keep kids engaged while providing actionable metrics to families and clinicians. Design rendering of the prototype and the MotorMate toy at Physical Therapy clinic for testing shown in the images to the right.
