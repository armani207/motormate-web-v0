Privacy model

A privacy model for a business network, not a scraped directory.

MotorMate Health uses Google-based intake and de-identified analytics to support catalog networking, product feedback, pilot outreach, and business development without turning community data into an uncontrolled directory.

What we collect

Intake may include contact details, role, location, catalog consent, organization type, CP experience, product interest, and pilot or partnership intent.

2

How we use it

We use submitted information to route sales, partnership, pilot, catalog, research, and community follow-up.

3

How we protect it

Public display is consent-based. Analytics should be aggregated or de-identified by default, and sensitive details are not published without explicit permission.

Public display rule

The catalog should inform, not expose.

Public-facing catalog and map experiences are structured around minimal display, role/location context, and opt-in participation. If a detail is not needed for discovery, it should not be public by default.
